## Claims and gaps (these are reports, not implemented SBT test results)

- **Q1**: palm/finger is the conventional comparator; wrist and textile fingers may differ. No documented finished SBT glove performance.
- **Q2**: constant-voltage DC (~0.5 V) classical measurement baseline; AC/polarity switching may reduce polarization drift. The numerical conditions are literature context, not an approved safe human-use circuit.
- **Q3**: raw EDA supports nonspecific sudomotor signal observations; it does not directly diagnose organ disease or nutritional status. Sample sizes, error and reference methods incomplete.
- **Q4**: no finished glove-specific Deep Research PDF archived in the inspected folder. PRIORITY P0.
- **Q5a/Q5b**: two runs of *the same* calibrated-resistor/RC-phantom query. Long report cites EmotiBit (2024) and a 2020 programmable EDA simulator, but performance claims and complete methods require independent audit. The short report includes a questionable direct connection between a seasonal-affective-disorder paper and resistor calibration; DO NOT treat citation numbers as validated. No traceable complete RC phantom standard identified in the retrieved reports. All suggested ≤1% accuracy and similar pass/fail limits remain **candidate project criteria**, not published consensus or measured SBT performance.
- **Q6**: proposal for peripheral monotonic timestamps, sequence numbers, local buffering, sample-gap flags and later transfer is useful. The report itself says it failed to retrieve official Android and Apple platform rules. Independently available primary docs: [Android BLE background](https://developer.android.com/develop/connectivity/bluetooth/ble/background) and [Apple Core Bluetooth background](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/NetworkingInternetWeb/Conceptual/CoreBluetooth_concepts/CoreBluetoothBackgroundProcessingForIOSApps/PerformingTasksWhileYourAppIsInTheBackground.html). OS-specific behavior must still be implemented and tested.
- **Q7**: regulatory claims depend on intended purpose and official EU legal texts. The PDF bibliography includes non-legal studies; no complete authoritative source-by-source applicability audit has been performed. Primary anchor: [EU MDR 2017/745 consolidated at 2026-01-01](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02017R0745-20260101).
- **Bibliographic evidence vs PDF counts:** a DOI-shaped string and a LeapSpace confidence label are not independent DOI/method/result verification. Separately verify study identity, original measurement methods and exact source-to-claim traceability.
