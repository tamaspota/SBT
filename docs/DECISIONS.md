D-006 (2026-11-03): Prepare independent LeapSpace research questions on EDA validation, front-end and wearable alternatives before selecting final hardware. No AI research output is accepted as factual until primary sources/DOIs are checked.

D-007 (2026-11-03): Mobile software will proceed in gated slices (simulation → bench BLE → real device → optional professional workflow). No full-stack or cloud platform before accepted paid pilot; avoid proprietary ZYTO algorithms and unverified medical claims.
